QUESTION: Which fluid compartment contains the largest proportion of total body water in a healthy adult?
A: Intracellular fluid
B: Intravascular fluid
C: Interstitial fluid
D: Transcellular fluid
ANSWER: A
RATIONALE: Intracellular fluid (ICF) contains the largest proportion of total body water because most body water is located inside cells. Intravascular and interstitial fluid are components of extracellular fluid and contain less water than the intracellular compartment. Transcellular fluid, such as cerebrospinal and synovial fluid, makes up only a small portion of total body water.
END

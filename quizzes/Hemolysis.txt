QUESTION: A patient with hemolytic anemia reports increasing fatigue and shortness of breath when walking to the bathroom. Which finding best explains these symptoms?
A: Increased platelet production
B: Reduced oxygen-carrying capacity
C: Increased plasma protein production
D: Increased white blood cell production
ANSWER: B
RATIONALE: Hemolysis decreases the number of circulating RBCs and can reduce hemoglobin, lowering oxygen-carrying capacity and causing fatigue and exertional dyspnea. Increased platelet or white blood cell production does not explain these symptoms, and increased plasma protein production does not restore oxygen transport.
END

RATIONALE: Intravascular hemolysis releases free hemoglobin into the bloodstream, which can be filtered into the urine and produce hemoglobinuria with dark or reddish-brown urine. Glucose, ketones, and sodium do not explain the characteristic urine finding from intravascular RBC destruction.
